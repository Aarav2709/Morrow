# Code of conduct

Morrow is made by people of every age, background and level of experience,
many of them helping for the first time. Be kind, be patient, and assume good
intent.

- Disagree with ideas, never attack people.
- No harassment, insults, or unwelcome attention, in issues, pull requests,
  discussions or anywhere else Morrow is talked about.
- Help newcomers find their way rather than pointing out what they got wrong.

If someone makes Morrow's spaces unwelcoming, contact the maintainer through
the email on their GitHub profile. Reports are kept private. Anyone who doesn't
follow this may be asked to stop, or removed from the project.
